Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about appointments, clinic timings, treatments, and procedures at Dentia.

You can book an appointment in three simple ways: (1) Use the online "Book Appointment" form on this website, (2) Call our clinic directly at +91 98765 43210, or (3) Message us on WhatsApp. Our patient desk will promptly confirm your preferred date and specialist time slot.

Dentia Clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM continuously. On Sundays, we are open from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM for scheduled appointments and urgent dental pain relief. Emergency dental care is accessible 24/7 on call.

Our clinic is centrally situated at 204-206, Harmony Health Plaza, Opposite Star Bazaar, Adajan Main Road, Surat, Gujarat - 395009. We offer ample free basement car and two-wheeler parking and complete wheelchair elevator accessibility.

We provide full-spectrum dental care: Dental Implants, Single-Sitting Root Canals, Laser Teeth Whitening, Clear Aligners (Invisalign), Ceramic & Metal Braces, Ultrasonic Cleaning & Polishing, Wisdom Tooth Extractions, Zirconia Crowns, Fixed Bridges, Pediatric Dentistry, Laser Gum Care, and Porcelain Veneers.

The Indian Dental Association and global standards recommend a preventive checkup and professional scaling every 6 months. Regular visits prevent minor plaque accumulation from advancing into painful tooth decay, nerve infections, or bone-loss periodontal disease.

While walk-in patients and emergency toothaches are always welcomed and attended to without delay, we strongly recommend scheduling an advance appointment to guarantee zero waiting time and direct consultation with your chosen dental specialist.
